# Product Angle Matrix — 1 product → 10 content angles

Use when a single affiliate product needs many ads/posts without repeating
itself. Each angle is a different *reason to watch*, not a re-cut of the
same video. Pick the angle first, then the hook formula, then the visuals.

Source: "สินค้า 1 ตัว ทำได้ถึง 10 คอนเทนต์" carousel (user reference, 2026-09-28).

## The 10 angles

| # | Angle | Viewer question it answers | Needs real product/person? | Faceless-friendly build |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unboxing | What do I actually get for the price? | Product in hand (library pack shots OK) | Pack shots + value math card (e.g. 1 แถม 1 = ฿40/ชิ้น) |
| 2 | First impression | What is it like the first time? | Yes, a real first try | Only with real footage; otherwise skip |
| 3 | How to use | How do I use it so it works? | Demo footage | Step cards + "3 mistakes" framing, drawn diagrams |
| 4 | Problem → solution | Why is my X bad and what fixes it? | No | Pain footage → reveal (hair ad #1 v5 is this) |
| 5 | Review after real use | Does it hold up after days? | **Yes, real use over time** | Never fake with TTS; needs the creator's own footage |
| 6 | Key features | What does it do that others don't? | No | 3/5-point feature cards, icon animation |
| 7 | Who it's for | Is this for someone like me? | No | "ผู้ชาย 3 แบบที่ควรมี…" persona vignettes |
| 8 | Comparison | Is it better than what I use now? | No | Tier list / versus board vs old methods; no body before/after |
| 9 | Lifestyle / daily use | Where does it fit in my day? | Light | "1 วันของ…" / morning-routine montage with a clock |
| 10 | Sell without selling | (entertains first) | No | POV skit/story; product appears as the natural fix |

## Rules when applying it

- **One test variable.** When several angles are tested, keep the voice, length, and offer line the same, so the angle is the only thing that changes (see [tiktok-prepost-checklist](tiktok-prepost-checklist.md)).
- **Honesty.**
  - Angles 2 and 5 are first-person experience. A TTS voice or AI persona must not claim "I used it for 7 days". Either shoot real footage or drop the angle.
  - POV skits (angle 10) are fine when framed as a scenario ("POV:"), not a testimonial.
- **Brand-controlled facts.**
  - Only state prices and offers the listing currently shows.
  - No health, medical, or body before/after claims (TikTok shadowban risk).
  - Comparisons are against *methods* (gel vs powder), not a person's body.
- **Hook formula per angle.** Pair each angle with a winning pattern:
  - Negative/command ("อย่า…", "มึงใช้ผิด…")
  - Number ("3 เหตุผล…", "จัดอันดับ…")
  - POV ("POV: …")
- **Cross-sell awareness.** Don't trash a product the same account also sells. Example: hair spray sits in a good tier in a hair-powder tier list.
- **Visuals.** Hook sets are designed per ad ([design-own-hooks memory]). Punch FX come from `remotion-composer/src/fxkit`.

## Output per angle (for approval before production)

1. The angle, and the hook line in the account's voice.
2. The VO script, 5–7 lines at 20–30 s. Show it to the user before generating TTS.
3. A shot plan: library footage IDs, drawn cutaways, and a Nano Banana image only if it is really needed.
4. What the angle tests, and anything it can't claim.
